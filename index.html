Recap Autopilot

Recap Autopilot is SweepCo's private publishing tool. It runs on our own computer and turns our recorded market recaps into edited videos and short clips. It uploads them to the SweepCo YouTube channel, schedules them for publishing, and reports on how they perform.

It's used only for SweepCo's own channel and isn't offered to the public.
