Privacy policy

Effective November 3, 2026

Recap Autopilot is a private tool that SweepCo uses to publish to its own YouTube channel. It isn't offered to the public, and the only Google account connected to it is SweepCo's own.

YouTube API Services

Recap Autopilot uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

With permission from the channel owner, Recap Autopilot can:

  • Upload videos, thumbnails and captions to the SweepCo channel
  • Set when those videos are published
  • Post comments on those videos
  • Read analytics for those videos, such as views, retention and subscribers gained

How data is used and stored

This data is used only to publish SweepCo's videos and to report on how they perform. Login tokens, analytics and run logs are stored on SweepCo's own computer. The tool has no server of its own and collects no data from viewers.

Who data is shared with

Data from YouTube's APIs isn't sold, or shared with anyone for advertising. Separately from that data, recap transcripts and generated post text are processed by these services:

  • Anthropic (Claude): transcripts of SweepCo's own recordings are sent to write titles, descriptions and clip selections.
  • Upload-Post: finished clips and captions are sent to schedule posts on other social platforms.
  • Telegram: a summary of each video is sent to SweepCo's owner for approval.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Locally stored data is deleted by removing it from SweepCo's computer.
